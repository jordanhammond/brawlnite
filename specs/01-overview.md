# 01 · Overview

## Vision
- ✅ **Look:** blocky, bright characters, like Roblox
- ✅ **Abilities:** every hero has a main attack and a charged super, like Brawl Stars
- ✅ **Gameplay:** drop in, fight, and be the last one standing, like Fortnite
- ✅ **No building:** pre-made worlds only

## Platform
- ✅ Plays on a **Chromebook Plus** in Chrome
- ✅ Plays locally, with no install and no internet needed (single HTML file)
- ✅ Also hosted online for friends, with shared high scores (see 14)
- 🔧 Also works on any desktop browser (Mac, Windows)

## Version 1 scope (MVP)

**In:**
- 1 human player vs. 9 bots (10 heroes in total)
- 4 starter heroes: Brickster, Zippy, Boomer, Pizza Pete
- 4 unlockable heroes (one per win): Shadow Sam, Frosty, Chomp, Barf Bush
- 4 secret heroes, hidden until all 8 are unlocked: Boo, Inky, Twister, Glaxo (the last)
- 1 map: Lava Volcano
- 4 pickups: Strength, Speed, Health, Invisibility
- Shrinking storm
- 3 bot difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, Hard
- Menu, hero select, pause, and win/lose screens
- Simple sound effects

**Out (for now; see 11-future-ideas):**
- Multiplayer (split screen or Wi-Fi)
- Building
- More maps and heroes
- Skins
- Music

## Success criteria
- The boys can pick a hero and finish a match without help
- A match lasts about **2–4 minutes**
- It runs smoothly (aiming for 60 fps) on the Chromebook Plus
- Each hero feels different to play
