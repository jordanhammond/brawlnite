# 03 · Heroes

All heroes are **chunky, blocky figures** (Roblox build, Brawl Stars attitude): box legs, torso, arms, and a big head.

✅ **Shared face style (from the boys' art):** a dark/black face with **big white angry eyes** and heavy brows, Brawl Stars style. (Barf Bush is the exception: a wooden face.)

🖼️ Reference images go in `art/heroes/<hero-name>/`

## Stat summary 🔧

| Hero | HP | Speed | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🧱 Brickster | 200 (high) | 6 (slow) | Tank |
| ⚡ Zippy | 100 (low) | 10 (fast) | Hit-and-run |
| 🎯 Boomer | 130 (medium) | 7.5 (medium) | Long range, area damage |
| 🍃 Barf Bush | 180 (medium-high) ✅ | 9 (fast) | Mid range, sprays groups |

*Speed is in map units per second. A hero is about 2.6 units tall.*

## 🧱 Brickster: the tank
🖼️ `art/heroes/brickster/`

**Look ✅ (from art)**
- Big **yellow hard hat** with a red brick logo on the front
- Black face, white angry eyes
- Yellow and orange armour, grey arm pads, brown gloves and boots
- **Backpack made of red, orange and grey bricks**
- Chunky yellow blaster with a grey barrel, wrench on his belt

**Attack: Blaster ✅**
- ✅ Fires a flaming **brick** straight ahead, with a fiery trail
- 🔧 22 damage · range 26 · fires every 0.45 seconds

**Super: Brick Fort Shield ✅** (the art shows a fort; there's no building, so it's temporary)
- ✅ A ring of red and grey brick blocks **pops up around him**, with dust and a "!" flash
- 🔧 Blocks **all** damage for 4 seconds, then the bricks crumble and fly apart
- He can still move and attack while shielded

## ⚡ Zippy: the speedster
🖼️ `art/heroes/zippy/`

**Look ✅ (from art)**
- Round **yellow helmet** with a big **lightning-bolt fin** on the side
- **Blue goggles** with glowing cyan lenses
- Black face, white eyes
- Blue and yellow suit, lightning-bolt emblem on the chest, blue gloves and boots
- Flowing **yellow scarf**

**Attack: Zap Burst ✅**
- ✅ Fires 3 quick blue lightning zaps **from his fists**, with slight spread
- 🔧 12 damage each (36 if all hit) · short range 13 · fires every 0.5 seconds

**Super: Dash ✅** (changed from "smash walls")
- Rockets forward about 14 units in 0.35 seconds, leaving a blue lightning trail
- 🔧 Every enemy he passes through takes 35 damage and gets **knocked back**
- He stops early if he hits a rock

## 🎯 Boomer: the long-range one
🖼️ `art/heroes/boomer/`

**Look ✅ (from art)**
- **Green army helmet** with a red star badge and **orange goggles**
- Black face, white eyes, **orange scarf** over the mouth
- Green outfit, orange and yellow cuffs, brown gloves and boots
- **Backpack full of black bombs** with lit fuses

**Attack: Bouncing Bomb ✅**

- ✅ It **bounces** once, then explodes
- 🔧 30 damage in a 3.5-unit blast · lands up to 18 units away · fires every 0.9 seconds

**Super: Big Rocket ✅** (changed from "blow up forts")
- ✅ A **big red rocket with a yellow star** and a fiery trail flies straight ahead and explodes on impact

## 🍃 Barf Bush: the leaf monster ✅ (designed by the boys) · 🔒 unlocks at 4 wins (the 8th hero)
🖼️ `art/heroes/barf-bush/`

**Look ✅ (from art)**
- A **round bush of leaves** in many shades of green
- **Brown wooden face** peeking out, with angry white eyes and a big open mouth with chunky teeth
- **Twig "horns"** sticking out of the head
- Brown wooden arms and legs covered in leaf clumps, big leafy fists and feet

**Attack: Leaf Barf ✅**
- A glowing stream of leaves comes out of the mouth, like vomit 🤮
- It's a cone shape, so it hits **everyone** inside it
- ✅ 26 damage per enemy · **range 14** (was 8: the boys said it was too short) · 35° cone · fires every 0.6 seconds

**Super: Leaf Laser ✅**
- A massive beam of leaves shoots out of the eyes
- ✅ It lasts **5 seconds**
- 🔧 60 damage per second to anyone in the beam · beam length 25
- 🔧 Barf Bush can turn while firing and moves at half speed

**Style ✅:** fast, medium health

**Playtest change log ✅**
- 2026-10-03: HP 140 → 180 (he died too quickly), and the leaf barf range went from 8 to 14 to match the other heroes

## 🦖 Chomp: the dino brawler

## 🍕 Pizza Pete: the trickster
🖼️ `art/heroes/pizza-pete/`

- **Attack: Pizza Fling ✅:** flings a spinning pizza slice that **bounces off rocks** (🔧 up to 2 bounces) · 🔧 20 damage · range 22 · every 0.55 s

  - ✅ Enemies on it get **stuck in cheese** (🔧 −75% speed, 8 damage/s)
- **Style ✅:** medium everything, heals himself

- **Super: Tornado ✅:** a tornado roams for **5 s**, chasing the nearest enemy, and **carries enemies away** · 🔧 20 damage/s while carried; they're dropped when it ends

## 🌌 Glaxo: the space hero ✅ (Jordan's design, the 12th and last unlock)

- **Attack: Space Stars ✅:** fast, glowing stars · 🔧 18 damage · range 24 · every 0.45 s
- **Super: Black Hole ✅:** a small black hole appears on the ground (🔧 6 units ahead, or on a bot's target)
  - ✅ **Any hero who steps on it is absorbed: instant elimination**
  - 🔧 It lasts only **3 s** and has a gentle pull (radius 5)
  - 🔧 Brickster's Brick Fort protects against it
  - 🔧 Bots try to steer around black holes
