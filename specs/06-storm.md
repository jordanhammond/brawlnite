# 06 · Storm ✅

A purple storm wall shrinks in phases, like in Fortnite. Standing outside the circle hurts.

## Phases 🔧

| Phase | Wait before shrinking | Shrink time | New radius | Damage outside |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | n/a | n/a | 95 (whole island) | n/a |
| 1 | 25 s | 25 s | 55 | 4 HP/s |
| 2 | 20 s | 20 s | 30 | 8 HP/s |
| 3 | 15 s | 15 s | 12 | 15 HP/s |
| 4 | 10 s | 15 s | 0 | 25 HP/s |

Total time until the storm closes completely is about 2 min 25 s.

## Centre 🔧
- The final centre is a random spot 25–40 units from the middle, never inside the volcano or a rock
- Each new circle always fits **inside** the previous one

## Visuals and HUD 🔧
- A tall, see-through purple wall shows where the edge is
- The minimap shows the current circle (purple) and the next circle (white)
- The top of the screen says "Storm shrinks in 0:15" or "Storm shrinking!"
- A warning appears when the player is outside the circle
