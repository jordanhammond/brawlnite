# 09 · Screens and Audio

## Screens 🔧
1. **Main menu / hero select**
   - BRAWLNITE logo
   - Wins record: "🏆 Wins · Games · Eliminations" (see 12)
   - Bot difficulty buttons: Easy / Normal / Hard
   - Big **▶ PLAY** button (above the cards so it fits on a Chromebook screen)
   - 8 hero cards: emoji, name, HP/speed/range bars, attack and super text. Locked ones are greyed out with 🔒.
   - Click a card to select it (it gets a gold outline)
   - A controls cheat sheet at the bottom
2. **Pause** (Esc, the ⏸ button on touch, or when the mouse unlocks): Resume / Quit to menu
3. **End**
   - Win: ✅ "🏆 YOU WON!" plus "Last one standing!"
   - Lose: "ELIMINATED" plus "You placed #4 · 2 eliminations"
   - Win progress or "🎉 4 NEW HEROES UNLOCKED!"
   - Buttons: Play again (same hero) / Change hero

## Victory dance ✅
When the player wins, their hero does a victory dance (the boys' request, 2026-10-03).
- 🔧 The camera swings round to the **front** of the hero and gently sways side to side
- 🔧 Confetti bursts out, then keeps sprinkling while the hero dances
- 🔧 One of 3 dances is picked at random each win:
  - **Flap:** arms flap up and down while hopping
  - **Spin:** arms out, spinning on the spot
  - **Sway:** hands in the air, swaying side to side and kicking
- 🔧 Floating heroes (Boo, Inky) dance without leg moves
- 🔧 The win panel appears after 2.5 sec (was 1.2), at the **bottom** of the screen with a see-through background, so the dance keeps going behind it
- 🔧 The hero can't be hurt while dancing (storm or lava)

## Audio 🔧
- Sounds are made in code (Web Audio), so there are no sound files
- Sounds: shoot (a different one per hero), hit, explosion, barf splat, laser hum, pickup chime, elimination, win fanfare, lose, "heroes left" alert
- Volume is kept low and friendly
- No music in version 1
