# 12 · Wins and Unlocks ✅

## Win tracking ✅
- The game counts **wins** (also 🔧 games played and total eliminations)
- 🔧 Saved in the browser's local storage on that device, so it survives closing Chrome
- ✅ Tracked **per player** (see 13-players.md)
- 🔧 Shown on the menu: "🏆 Wins: 2 · Games: 5"
- 🔧 Reset for testing: open the game with `?resetwins` on the end of the address (current player only)

## Starter heroes ✅
🧱 Brickster · ⚡ Zippy · 🎯 Boomer · 🍕 Pizza Pete

## Unlocks: one hero per win ✅
| Wins | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 1 | 🥷 Shadow Sam 🔧 |
| 2 | 🧊 Frosty 🔧 |
| 3 | 🦖 Chomp 🔧 |
| 4 | 🍃 Barf Bush ✅ (the 8th hero) |

- 🔧 Locked cards are greyed out with "🔒 Win 2 matches to unlock (1/2)" and can't be selected
- 🔧 After a win that unlocks someone, the end screen shows "🎉 NEW HERO UNLOCKED: 🥷 Shadow Sam!"
- 🔧 Otherwise it shows "Next hero in 1 win!"
- 🔧 **Bots can use any unlocked or locked hero** in tiers 1 and 2, so the boys see what they're working toward

## Tier 3: secret heroes ✅
- ✅ 4 more heroes are **hidden from the hero list** until all 8 are unlocked (4 wins)
- 🔧 They then unlock one per win:

| Wins | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 5 | 👻 Boo 🔧 |
| 6 | 🐙 Inky 🔧 |
| 7 | 🌪️ Twister 🔧 |
| 8 | 🌌 Glaxo ✅ (the very last: he's the most powerful) |

- 🔧 Bots don't use them until they're revealed, so they stay a surprise
- 🔧 Revealing them shows "🤫 4 SECRET HEROES REVEALED!" on the end screen
- Hero designs: see 03-heroes.md
