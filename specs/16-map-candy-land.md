# 16 · Map: Candy Land ✅

✅ The boys' second world (2026-10-04). It **unlocks at 8 wins**, once every hero is unlocked (see 12-wins-and-unlocks.md).

🖼️ Reference: `art/map/Candy Land Final Showdown.png`

## Same rules as Lava Volcano 🔧
- Same round island (radius ~90), storm, pickups, spawn ring and 10 heroes
- Only the look and the names change. Everything in 05-map-lava-volcano.md about lava, obstacles and launch pads still applies.

## Picking a world 🔧
- A **World:** row on the menu: 🌋 Lava Volcano · 🍭 Candy Land
- Before 8 wins it shows "🔒 Candy Land: win 8 (5/8)" and can't be picked
- The menu background changes to the picked world
- Online: the **host** picks the world in the waiting room, and everyone sees it there. Guests don't need it unlocked.
- After the 8th win, the end screen shows "🍭 NEW WORLD UNLOCKED: CANDY LAND!"

## The Candy Tree (the middle) ✅
- ✅ An enormous tree made of candy, instead of the volcano
- 🔧 Trunk: 6 twisted **candy-cane strands** in different stripes, splaying out at the top like branches
- 🔧 Top: about 75 giant **gumballs** plus swirly **lollipops** poking out
- 🔧 Roots: a melted-chocolate mound with a pile of sweets
- 🔧 Blocks heroes and shots like the volcano does, even when launched

## Hot chocolate ✅ (instead of lava)
- ✅ Molten hot chocolate **saps health**: 🔧 30 HP/sec, same as lava
- 🔧 10 pools with a **whipped-cream rim** and rising steam, plus a hot chocolate sea around the island
- 🔧 Messages: "☕ You're in hot chocolate!" and "… melted in the hot chocolate ☕"

## Big candy to hide behind ✅
All of these block movement and straight shots.
- 🔧 **Peppermint lollipops** (14): big swirl discs on striped sticks
- 🔧 **Gumdrops** (24): sugary domes in 7 colours (the "boulders")
- 🔧 **Chocolate truffles** (7): with sprinkles
- 🔧 **Chocolate cakes** (12): one or two layers with icing drips
- 🔧 **Candy canes** (8): giant, hook on top

## Bounce pads ✅
- ✅ Look like round **red-and-white peppermint candies** lying flat, slowly spinning
- 🔧 Pink pulse ring and red/white sparkles when you bounce
- Same 6 spots and same jump as Lava Volcano's pads

## Look and feel 🔧
- **Pink frosting ground** with sprinkles. 🔧 The art has chocolate ground, but pink makes the hot chocolate easy to spot.
- Island edge: chocolate cake with pink icing drips, and a **biscuit-stick fence**
- Pink, lilac and peach **mountains with frosting caps**
- Blue-to-pink sky, **cotton candy clouds**, and **sprinkles** floating down
